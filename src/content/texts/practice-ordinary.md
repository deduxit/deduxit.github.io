---
title: Practice, ordinary logic
---
Each problem is a sequent to decide in its logic, classical, intuitionistic or minimal: prove
it, or find that it has none. Choose the kinds and the difficulty you want, or let chance
choose. A problem opens in a new tab of the page.

A problem counts as proved when your steps close every goal. Where a goal has no proof, Close
goal says so and draws a countermodel.
