title: About
---
This page is [deduxit](https://github.com/deduxit/deduxit)'s prover for
linear logic and for ordinary propositional logic in LK and LJ, running in your browser: you type a sequent and prove it step by
step, or let deduxit's search close a goal. deduxit's library is compiled to
WebAssembly and runs in the page; nothing you type is sent anywhere, and your
proofs stay in this browser's storage.

deduxit's [manual](/deduxit/manual/) describes the command line prover and its
benchmarks, the library's documentation is at [/deduxit/](/deduxit/), and their
source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/deduxit/deduxit). For AI
models, [llms.txt](/llms.txt) says what this page is and where deduxit's guide for
them is.
Questions, mistakes and wishes are welcome as
[issues](https://github.com/deduxit/deduxit.github.io/issues).
