Every problem here has a proof; Learn's lessons hold the ones without. Choose
the kinds and the difficulty you want, or let chance choose. A problem opens in
a new tab of the page, beside the sequent field. Hint helps when you are stuck.

A problem counts as proved when you close every goal yourself; steps Hint
applied count, goals that Close or Close all finished do not. Difficulty counts
the steps a proof by hand takes and the choices on the way.
