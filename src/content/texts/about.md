title: About
---
On this page you prove sequents of linear logic, and of ordinary propositional
logic in the sequent calculi LK and LJ. Type a sequent, then prove it step by step
or let the search close a goal. The prover is
[deduxit](https://github.com/deduxit/deduxit)'s library, compiled to WebAssembly
and run in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere, and your proofs stay
in this browser's storage.

deduxit's [manual](/deduxit/manual/) describes its command-line prover and its
benchmarks, the library's documentation is at [/deduxit/](/deduxit/), and their
source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/deduxit/deduxit). For AI
models, [llms.txt](/llms.txt) says what this page is and where deduxit's guide for
them is.
Questions, mistakes and wishes are welcome as
[issues](https://github.com/deduxit/deduxit.github.io/issues).
