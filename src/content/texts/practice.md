Every problem here has a proof; Learn's lessons hold the ones without. Choose
the kinds and the difficulty you want, or let chance choose. A problem opens in
a new tab of the page, beside the sequent field.

A problem counts as proved when your steps close every goal. Steps that Hint applies
count as yours; a goal that Close goal or Close all closes does not.
Difficulty counts the steps a proof by hand takes and the choices on the way.
