title: About
---
This page is [linlog](https://github.com/sequentium/sequentium)'s prover for
linear logic, running in your browser: you type a sequent and prove it step by
step, or let linlog's search close a goal. linlog's library is compiled to
WebAssembly and runs in the page; nothing you type is sent anywhere, and your
proofs stay in this browser's storage.

linlog's [manual](/sequentium/manual/) describes the command line prover and its
benchmarks, the library's documentation is at [/sequentium/](/sequentium/), and their
source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/sequentium/sequentium). For AI
models, [llms.txt](/llms.txt) says what this page is and where linlog's guide for
them is.
Questions, mistakes and wishes are welcome as
[issues](https://github.com/sequentium/sequentium.github.io/issues).
