deduxit's mark

The files of this directory are deduxit's mark: the word mark dēdūxit
(wordmark-light.svg, wordmark-dark.svg, wordmark-mono.svg, and
wordmark-band.svg at the page's band height), its square form dē
(square-light.svg, square-dark.svg, square-mono.svg), and the images drawn
from them (the favicon, the app icons, the Apple touch icon, the link
preview's image).

The name "deduxit" and its mark identify the deduxit project
(https://github.com/deduxit/deduxit), whose prover is linlog. They may not
be used in a way that suggests that the project or its author endorses
anything else.

The word is set in Gentium ExtraBold (Gentium 7.000, © 2003–2025 SIL
Global) and outlined, so no font file goes with these files. Gentium is
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, whose condition 5 exempts a document
created using the font from that licence; a logo of outlines is not a
modified version of the font, so the Reserved Font Names do not bind it.
